# AI product-market opportunity map for 2027

Last reviewed: 22 October 2026.

| Opportunity | Signal | Priority | Evidence-backed interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| AI integration and workflow engineering | Demonstrated demand | 95 | Upwork reports and live briefs specify integrations, automation, full-stack ownership, and production behavior rather than generic prompting. |
| Agent evaluation, reliability, and observability | Demonstrated demand | 93 | Engineering surveys and current roles repeatedly require evals, failure tracing, permissions, and regression evidence. |
| RAG, search, and governed knowledge | Demonstrated demand | 90 | Current briefs explicitly request RAG, citations, vector stores, and production testing. |
| Voice and realtime agents | Demonstrated demand | 88 | Voice roles emphasize latency, reliability, tool execution, deployment, and domain guardrails. |
| MCP, permissions, and agent enablement | Emerging demand with direct evidence | 86 | MCP is present in live briefs and role descriptions alongside connectors, identity, and scoped access. |
| Developer agents and controlled delivery | Demonstrated enterprise adoption | 85 | Coding-agent adoption is visible; durable value depends on controlled edits, tests, and review. |
| Human-gated industry agents | Validated need, uneven scaling | 84 | Enterprise adoption is expanding, but ROI and governance remain inconsistent; bounded workflows are the credible wedge. |
| Multimodal media and computer vision | Demonstrated and growing demand | 82 | Marketplace data shows strong image/video and vision growth; differentiation requires workflow and evaluation depth. |
| Browser/local privacy-preserving AI | Strategic 2027 opportunity | 74 | Local inference has compelling privacy and cost properties, but direct buyer demand is less visible than integration demand. |

The interactive `#/opportunities` page links each conclusion to its dated source record. This map is a decision aid, not a market-size forecast.
